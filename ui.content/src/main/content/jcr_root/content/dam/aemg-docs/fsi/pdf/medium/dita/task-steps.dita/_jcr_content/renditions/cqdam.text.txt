   Task Steps
     Once you have set up your vehicle maintenance system, you need to perform a series of tasks to ensure the vehicle is in top condition.
         Inspect
         Inspect the vehicle's engine, tires, and fluid levels to ensure everything is functioning properly before starting the maintenance process.
         Replace
         Replace old engine oil and filters, as well as worn-out brake pads, ensuring your vehicle's performance remains optimal.
         Test
         Test the battery, lights, and electronic systems to confirm all components are in good working order, reducing the risk of failures.
         Clean
         Clean the vehicle, both inside and out, to maintain its appearance and prevent wear and tear caused by dirt and grime accumulation.
