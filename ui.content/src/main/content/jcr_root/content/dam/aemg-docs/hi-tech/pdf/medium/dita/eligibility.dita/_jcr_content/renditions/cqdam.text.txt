   Eligible Categories
   The below table sets out the list of eligible categories for high-tech projects together with the eligibility criteria for each category and examples of any relevant exclusions.
       Eligible Categories
             Technology Area
             Hi-Tech Industry Category
             Eligibility Criteria
             Examples/Exclusions
             AI and Machine Learning
             Artificial Intelligence Solutions
                 Development and deployment of AI algorithms and systems
                 Advancements in machine learning models for predictive analytics, autonomous decision-making, and data processing
                 Technologies focused on enhancing AI capabilities across industries (e.g., healthcare, finance, autonomous vehicles)
                Examples:
                 Natural Language Processing (NLP) applications, Autonomous Vehicles (AI-driven systems), Smart Robotics, AI-powered healthcare diagnostics, AI chatbots, AI-enhanced cybersecurity solutions
                Exclusions, include, but are not limited to:
                 Projects with high carbon emissions due to inefficient energy usage in data processing centers
                 AI solutions that exacerbate data privacy issues without robust protection measures
             Cloud Computing
             Cloud Infrastructure and Services
                 Development, maintenance, and expansion of cloud infrastructure services
                 Technologies that enable scalable cloud platforms, storage solutions, and computational services
                 Adoption of green data centers and cloud services for energy-efficient computing
                Examples:
                 Cloud computing platforms (AWS, Microsoft Azure, Google Cloud), Edge computing solutions, Cloud-based SaaS applications, Energy-efficient data centers
                Exclusions, include, but are not limited to:
                 Cloud services running on non-renewable energy sources
                 Data centers with significant cooling inefficiencies or high carbon footprints
             Renewable Tech
             Green Tech Solutions for Clean Energy
                 Development of hardware and software that promotes renewable energy generation or energy storage
                 Smart grid technologies that optimize energy distribution using advanced algorithms
                 Energy-efficient devices for residential and commercial use (e.g., smart thermostats, energy-saving appliances)
                Examples:
                 Smart grids, Solar energy management systems, Energy storage solutions like batteries and supercapacitors, IoT-based energy monitoring devices
                Exclusions, include, but are not limited to:
                 Non-energy-efficient products that increase the overall demand for energy consumption
                 Technologies relying on non-renewable or pollutive energy sources
     All Eligible Assets will, to the extent applicable, adhere to industry sustainability and ethical technology standards, including regulations for data security, responsible AI, and green computing practices. We also ensure that technologies align with international agreements and standards on carbon neutrality and resource efficiency.
