 Eligible Assets Evaluation and Selection Process
   The Eligible Assets evaluation and selection process seeks to ensure that an amount equivalent to the net proceeds of any Hi-Tech Financing Transaction is allocated to assets qualifying as Eligible Assets in emerging technologies.
           The Rise of Digital Technologies
       The Evolution of High-Tech Industry
       The high-tech industry has experienced rapid growth and innovation, with advancements driven by digital transformation, AI, and automation. From early computing systems to the current era of quantum computing, the industry has consistently pushed the boundaries of what technology can achieve, changing the way we interact with the world.
       Early Innovations in Technology
       In the 20th century, the high-tech industry was defined by breakthroughs in computing, telecommunications, and semiconductors. Key milestones like the invention of the transistor, the first computer networks, and the development of microprocessors laid the foundation for the digital age.
       The Rise of Digital Technologies
       Digital technologies, such as personal computers, smartphones, and the internet, revolutionized how information is processed and shared. The continuous miniaturization of hardware and the explosion of digital platforms have shaped the modern tech landscape.
       Sustainability in Hi-Tech Product Design
       As the world shifts towards sustainability, the tech industry is innovating to reduce its environmental impact. From energy-efficient hardware to eco-friendly manufacturing processes, companies are integrating green technologies into product design to minimize e-waste and power consumption.
       Environmental Impact of Tech Products
       While high-tech products like smartphones and laptops offer immense utility, they also contribute to electronic waste (e-waste) and energy consumption. The industry is increasingly focused on recycling, energy-efficient components, and sustainable materials to mitigate environmental impact.
       Transition to Green Technologies
       To tackle environmental challenges, tech companies are investing in renewable energy sources, sustainable manufacturing, and circular economy practices. Innovations in solar-powered devices, low-energy processors, and recyclable materials are examples of the industry's shift to green technologies.
       The Shift Toward Smart Devices
       Smart devices are at the forefront of the tech revolution, with innovations like Internet of Things (IoT), wearable tech, and connected ecosystems transforming daily life. These devices are designed to enhance productivity, health, and convenience while minimizing environmental impact through energy efficiency.
       Growth of the Hi-Tech Market
       The global high-tech market is expanding rapidly as digital transformation accelerates across industries. Advancements in AI, cloud computing, 5G connectivity, and automation are driving unprecedented growth, with industries such as healthcare, finance, and manufacturing adopting cutting-edge technologies to improve efficiency and outcomes.
       Government Regulations and Support
       Governments around the world are implementing policies and regulations to foster innovation in the high-tech sector. This includes funding for research and development, tax incentives for sustainable tech, and regulations aimed at data security, privacy, and ethical AI use. These efforts are helping accelerate the adoption of new technologies and driving economic growth in the tech industry.
